BayesAI endorses an array of meetings and events around the world.

BayesAI organizes a series of workshops on Bayesianism in the Age of Modern AI, and sessions at other meetings (e.g. the ISBA World Meeting), and endorses events, small meetings, or workshops (e.g. at NeurIPS).

The call for proposals for future editions of the Bayes AI meetings usually opens at the event. All future editions are expected to adhere to the clauses for the Bayes AI meetings.

Endorsed meetings: BayesAI-ISBA supports and endorses other meetings, sessions, workshops to encourage activity in Bayesian AI. To request endorsement, please submit a single PDF file to bayesai.isba.section@gmail.com, containing information on the event including Title, Dates, Location, Website (if available), Description, and BayesAI-ISBA representation (any involvement of BayesAI-ISBA members).
