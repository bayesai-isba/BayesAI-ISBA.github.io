BayesAI-ISBA. The Bayesian Artificial Intelligence Section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis.

BayesAI-ISBA is a section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis (ISBA) aiming to foster interaction and collaboration between the Bayesian statistics and AI communities and promote the research, application, and dissemination of Bayesian inference in machine learning and AI.

To this aim, we organize a series of Meetings on Bayesian AI. Additionally, we hold other occasional workshops and sessions at other major conferences.

Join BayesAI-ISBA

Are you interested in Bayesian AI? Would you like to participate in activities to promote your research and learn about the latest research in Bayesian AI? Join BayesAI!

We welcome researchers at any level. To become a BayesAI-ISBA member, subscribe to ISBA here. In the memberships form you will have the possibility to add your one-year membership to BayesAI-ISBA (Bayesian AI Section) for only $5 more, or even a lifetime membership for $50.

To stay up-to-date, sign up to our BayesAI-ISBA google group. Please include your Name and Affiliation when requesting to join.

Events

We organize the annual Bayesianism in the Age of Modern AI workshop series. It started in 2024 at Dagstuhl, and continued in 2025 at MBZUAI. The third workshop will be hosted by the University of Edinburgh in 2026, and it will be the first to be organized by the BayesAI-ISBA Section.
